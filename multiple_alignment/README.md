Multiple Alignment is done to find out the conserved and variable regions and also to compare the selected sequences from same gene in different species. 

# Tools Used 
- MEGA (CLUSTALW)

# Steps 
1. Open MEGA and go to align section.
2. Select or paste the different sequences that needs to be aligned.
3. Click on the CLUSTALW option.
4. Your selected sequences are now aligned.

# Conclusion
Most of the regions are conserved that means they remained same during the course of evolution of different species but some are variable regions that includes gaps due to insertion or deletion that may have changed due to different reasons during the evolution. Overall, the alignment provided a basis for comparing the selected species and was further used for phylogenetic analysis to determine their evolutionary relationships by construction of phylogenetic tree.
